# Halloween 2026 Lore

Some info:

- The theme is **eldritch cosmic horror**, since there is no solid theme for spawn
- Lore are followed individually
- Plugin used: Headblocks, FancyNPC

## How the player follow the story

Players are tasked with following a lost journal in the mines. Each journal they found, they will get a lore book and a hint where to find the next journal, making them following a currated trail of lost journal entries.

To do the logic for the lost journals, we use the headblocks hunt to place the journal and hid them throughout the small outposts/landmarks. To make sure players follow the order of the journal correctly, the headblocks hunt will be done in an ordered manner with the order feature.

## Story Location

### Archeology Outpost
An outpost used by the archeologist investigating the weird fossiles found in the mines.

### Mines
A mine of a strange ore that befouled scientists. A few years after the mine were established, they uncovered strange fossils of a creature that were never recorded in any books.

#### Surface Mine
The shallow portion of the mine that the Phase 1 of the story occour in. 

#### Deep Cavern
The cavern area that were hidden behind the cobwebs. The archeology team set up various minor outposts in the deep cavern to study the fossils and strange ore found in the mountain.

#### Heart of the Plague
The source of the strange fossils and ore in the mountain, the corpse of an eldritch being that rots and corrupted the mountain.

## General Storyline

### Phase 0 - Gossip

NPCs around the spawn talk about what happend in the mines.

### Phase 1 - The Mines

Players found a new archeology room in `/spawn` mines.

There will be NPCs in there investigating what happend to the mining and archeology team. Players will be asked to piece together what happend to the mines.

Players are tasked to go inside the mines to gather information.

At the mines, player gather small journal notes

After entering the secret enterance, player will found out that, the archeology team found an entrance to an even deeper cave system and have explored it thoroughly.
