- The theme is **eldritch cosmic horror**, since there is no solid theme for spawn
- Lore are followed individually
- Plugin used: Headblocks, FancyNPC

## How the player follow the story

Players are tasked with following a lost journal in the mines. Each journal they found, they will get a lore book and a hint where to find the next journal, making them following a currated trail of lost journal entries.

To do the logic for the lost journals, we use the headblocks hunt to place the journal and hid them throughout the small outposts/landmarks. To make sure players follow the order of the journal correctly, the headblocks hunt will be done in an ordered manner with the order feature.

## Chronological Storyline

In a seemingly normal day in the world, a story broke out from a remote island that shock the world. Fossils and corpses started to surface from the seas around that remote island. And a hematite mine found a new ore they named "Quietus Ore". A red ore that grew among the hematite deposits.

After a few years of mining, they found weird fossils deep within the mountain. They uncovered strange fossils of a creature that were never recorded in any encyclopedia. An archeologist by the name of XXX were tasked to investigate the fossils that were found at that mountain along with a group of miners to dug deeper into the mines. 

From the knowledge that he have, he warns the institute about going deeper to the mines. He once read that there was a record of a folklore about creatures that roam in the caverns of those mines. Judging the freshness of the bones, he worries that there might be some of those creature still exists within the mountain.

With that warning, the institute still insists on doing an expedition and explore the mountain thoroughly. They sailed to the island once everything prepared.

When approacing the island they starts to notice lots of weird fish and huge skulls bones, and spikes all around the water.

After they arrived at the island they set up an outpost near the entrance of the mine and began studying what the miner have found. They also set up some archeology equipment to study the strange fossils they found in the cavern.

After days of investigating, the miner team made a breakthrough, they found a cave entrance that leads to a cave system under the mountain.

At first the archeology team ask for a new team of explorers to help them explore the caves and kill any monsters in that cave, but the institute pressured them to explore slowly with caution, while the explorer team is sailing to that remote island.

### Phase 2

After entering the secret enterance, player will found out that, the archeology team found an entrance to an even deeper cave system and have explored it thoroughly. There are smaller outpost scattered around the deep mines and players have to go to it one by one. In each outpost theres a journal note that explain their findings.

The text journal seems normal at first but the deeper the archeology team goes, the weirder they become. They started to see vision and dreams of some being. And they started to feel an unknown pull towards the center of the mountain.

After they set up their last outpost (Outpost Charlie) they(the archeologist) refuse to go deeper and claims that they feel ominous yet welcoming feeling if they continue deeper. But the curiousity get to them and they eventually go deeper.

At the journey to the deepest part of the mine, they starts to hear more whispers and strange visions.

At one point, one of the team member collapsed. They stopped for a bit but the collapsed member insists that they keep going and leave him there. After they said that, 2 of the archeologist member also stays behind because they feel ill so its now just 3 archeologist including the professor that goes deep into the mines.

At the end of their journey, they finally reached the bottom of the mines.
